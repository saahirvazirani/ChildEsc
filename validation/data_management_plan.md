# Prospective Validation Data Management Plan

Status: protocol only; collection is not authorized

## Data minimization

If a future ethics determination permits validation, collect only:

- Study-specific participant ID.
- Broad reviewer role or age band required by the approved analysis.
- Rubric responses and optional comments.
- Consent/assent status in a separate access-controlled record.

Do not collect personal crisis histories, abuse disclosures, clinical records, precise location, school, employer, social-media identity, or unnecessary demographic detail.

## Separation and access

- Store contact/consent records separately from response data.
- Restrict identifiable records to authorized study staff.
- Give analysts de-identified exports whenever possible.
- Never include raw identifiable records in the benchmark, supplement, or model prompts.

## Retention and deletion

- Set retention periods in the approved protocol before collection.
- Honor withdrawal and deletion rules stated during consent.
- Destroy recruitment contact data as soon as operationally permitted.
- Document any data that cannot be withdrawn after aggregation.

## Reporting

- Suppress small cells that could identify participants.
- Paraphrase or omit comments that contain personal information.
- Report disagreement and missingness rather than forcing consensus.
- Publish rubrics, aggregate methods, and synthetic examples; do not publish participant-level sensitive data by default.

## Incident handling

- Stop collection if the approved platform, storage, or consent flow fails.
- Follow the approved safeguarding and institutional incident process for unexpected disclosures.
- Do not improvise emergency contact or mandatory-reporting decisions in the research software.
