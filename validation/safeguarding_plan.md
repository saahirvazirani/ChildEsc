# Prospective Youth-Review Safeguarding Plan

Status: template only; must be reviewed and approved by the responsible institution

## Roles required before recruitment

- Principal investigator responsible for the approved protocol.
- Named safeguarding lead with youth-facing training.
- Session facilitator trained on sensitive disclosures and study boundaries.
- Data steward responsible for access and deletion controls.
- Institutional contact for adverse events and protocol deviations.

## Before a session

- Confirm valid consent and assent.
- Reconfirm age, approved jurisdiction, and private-but-not-isolated participation conditions.
- Explain content, skip/stop rights, confidentiality limits, and support boundaries.
- Provide approved support information without presenting the research team as a crisis service.
- Verify that the facilitator knows the institution-specific escalation procedure.

## During a session

- Do not solicit personal experiences.
- If a participant begins a sensitive disclosure, acknowledge without probing, remind them of privacy limits, and follow the approved decision tree.
- Pause immediately for visible distress, confusion, or a request to stop.
- Do not independently contact caregivers, emergency services, or authorities outside the approved safeguarding and legal process.
- Record only the minimum incident information required by the approved protocol.

## After a session

- Conduct an age-appropriate debrief and neutral transition.
- Offer approved support resources.
- Document skips, withdrawals, adverse events, and protocol deviations without adding unnecessary personal detail.
- Follow the approved notification timeline for the safeguarding lead and institution.
- Provide researcher debrief and wellness support after difficult sessions.

## Stop conditions

Suspend the study if:

- Consent, assent, or confidentiality explanations fail.
- The safeguarding lead is unavailable.
- Participants repeatedly provide unanticipated personal disclosures.
- Materials cause distress beyond the approved risk assessment.
- Data access, storage, or deletion controls fail.
- A protocol deviation changes the risk profile.

Restart only after qualified institutional review.
