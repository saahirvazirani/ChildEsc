# Human-Validation Ethics Gate

Decision date: 2026-08-17

## Current determination

No institutional ethics determination, approved protocol, or authorized collaborating institution is documented in this workspace. Therefore:

- No practitioner recruitment or response collection is authorized as research data.
- No youth recruitment, assent, consent, or response collection is authorized.
- No participant quotation, agreement statistic, or validation claim may appear in the current submission.
- Practitioner and youth materials created here are prospective protocol artifacts only.

After the technical evidence freeze, one non-professional individual with
mental-health knowledge provided an incomplete informal editorial read of a
review worksheet. It was not conducted under the prospective practitioner
protocol and does not satisfy that protocol's role, sampling, independence, or
authorization requirements. It may inform wording and protocol design only.
Do not publish item-level responses, quotations, agreement statistics, or label
changes from it. This editorial feedback does not reopen the gate.

This is a conservative project control, not a legal or institutional determination. A qualified institutional reviewer must decide the applicable requirements.

## Evidence vocabulary

Until an approved and completed protocol exists, the manuscript may say:

- `A prospective practitioner and youth-participatory validation protocol is provided.`
- `No practitioner or youth validation has occurred.`
- `Child-centered is a design objective, not a measured property.`

It may not say:

- `practitioner-validated`
- `clinically validated`
- `youth-validated`
- `youth-informed`, if no youth participated
- `expert consensus`

## Requirements before practitioner data collection

- Institutional determination for the proposed activity.
- Recruitment and consent materials.
- Role, expertise, independence, and conflict criteria.
- Data minimization, retention, access, and deletion rules.
- Sensitive-content warning and opt-out.
- Compensation and researcher-wellness procedures.
- Analysis plan fixed before responses are inspected.

## Additional requirements before youth participation

- Explicit approval for research involving minors.
- Developmentally appropriate guardian consent and youth assent, unless an institution authorizes a different route.
- Safeguarding lead and escalation protocol.
- Clear handling of mandatory-reporting obligations and limits of confidentiality.
- No prompts soliciting personal crisis, abuse, or self-harm disclosures.
- Immediate withdrawal and distress procedures.
- Age-appropriate compensation, privacy, and data deletion processes.

## Gate reopening

This gate may be reopened only after a qualified human records the applicable approval or determination and confirms that the final materials match it. Protocol preparation alone does not reopen the gate.
